## 1. Introduction

Kelia ("the App") is a family digital assistant developed by Kelia Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why, where it is stored, and who can access it.

We have written it to describe what the App actually does. By using Kelia, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

---

## 2. Information You Provide

Kelia stores what you and your family choose to enter:

**Account**
Name, email address, and a password (encrypted — we never see it in plain text).

**Family**
Profiles of your family members: name, role, age, and birthday. These are entered by you, including profiles for children.

**Daily life**
Calendar events and appointments, tasks, shopping lists, home maintenance records, and budget entries.

**Chat**
Text messages, photos, documents, and voice messages exchanged with your family inside the App.

**Memories and Time Capsules**
Photos, videos, voice recordings, written messages, and the dates and people associated with them.

**Documents**
Titles, categories, expiry dates, and photos of documents you choose to store.

**Smart Shopping**
Store names, loyalty card numbers, an optional photo of the card, and — only if you choose to save it — the geographic position of a store (see section 6).

**Kelia Care** *(optional)*
If you use Kelia Care, you may enter medication names, dosages and schedules, medical appointments, care journal notes (written or voice), emergency contacts, and care-related expenses and documents. See section 7.

## 3. Information Collected Automatically

**Location** — used in three different ways, described fully in section 6.

**Usage activity** — Kelia records which features are used and some of the actions you take, in order to make the App more useful. Some of these records include the text you entered — for example the name of a product you ticked off a shopping list, or the title of a task you completed. This information stays within your own family space and is never shared outside it.

**Device language** — to display the App in your language.

## 5. Who Can See Your Data

Your family data is visible only to the people you have invited into your family or care circle. No other Kelia user can see it.

Within a family, not everyone needs the same access. The person who created a family or care circle can decide what each member is allowed to see. A professional caregiver, for example, can be given access to medication schedules and appointments without being able to see family finances, private documents, or personal memories.

These limits are enforced both in the App and at the database level, so information you have restricted is never sent to an unauthorised device in the first place.

## 6. Location

Kelia uses location in three distinct ways. All are optional, and before any location permission is requested the App shows a clear explanation of what will be collected and why.

### 6.1 Local weather

When you open Kelia, it may detect which city you are in to show local weather.

This position is used **only at that moment**. It is not stored on our servers and is not shared with anyone. If you decline, Kelia shows the weather for a default city.

### 6.2 Saved store locations

If you choose to save a store's position in Smart Shopping, Kelia records the coordinates of the point where you are standing and **stores them in your family space**.

These coordinates are kept until you delete them, and are used only to recognise that store later. You can delete a saved store position at any time within the App.

### 6.3 Proximity alerts — location in the background

If you turn on **"Notify me near stores"** in Settings, Kelia uses your location **even when the App is not open**, so it can notify you when you are near a store for which you have an active shopping list.

- This feature is **turned off by default**
- It requires your explicit consent, shown before any permission is requested
- It can be turned off at any time in Settings
- Your position is compared against your saved stores on your own device. We do not build or store a history of your movements

**Location is never used for advertising and is never sold or shared with third parties.**

## 7. Kelia Care — Health-Related Information

Kelia Care is an optional feature for families coordinating the care of a loved one.

If you use it, the information you enter — medications, dosages and times, appointments, care journal notes, emergency contacts — is stored in your care circle and is visible only to the people you have explicitly authorised, according to the permissions described in section 5.

**Kelia does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.** It organises information that you have entered yourself, and it does not replace guidance from a healthcare professional.

Kelia does not connect to Health Connect, Apple Health, or any device health sensors, and does not read health data from other apps.

This information is never shared with third parties and is never used for advertising.

## 8. AI Assistant

Kelia includes an AI assistant. When you send it a message, the App also sends a summary of your family context so the answer can be relevant.

**This context can include:**

- The names, roles, ages, and birthdays of your family members, including children
- Today's events, their times, and who they are assigned to
- Items on your shopping list, and which stores your lists are linked to
- Urgent home maintenance tasks
- A short summary of recurring habits Kelia has noticed in your family

Your message and this context are sent to **OpenRouter**, which generates the response and processes the data according to its own privacy policy. We do not permanently store your conversation history on our servers.

If you prefer not to share this information, simply do not use the AI assistant — the rest of Kelia works without it.

---

## 9. Invitations and Referrals

When you invite someone to your family, Kelia creates an invitation containing a randomly generated code. If you type in the person's email or phone number so they can be reached, that detail is stored with the invitation.

Kelia also records which member invited which new member. This is used to understand how families grow and, in future, to recognise members who bring others in. It is never shared outside your family space and is never sold.

You can view or revoke pending invitations at any time from the Family screen.

---

## 10. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored using **Supabase**, a cloud database and storage provider. All data is:

- Transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
- Protected by database-level rules ensuring only your own family members can access it
- Linked to your personal account

Photos, videos, voice recordings, documents, and loyalty card images are stored in encrypted cloud storage linked to your account.

## 16. How Long We Keep Your Data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Kelia does not delete it automatically after a set period.

You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time from within the App:

**Settings → Zona pericolosa ("Danger Zone") → Elimina account ("Delete account")**

This is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to sign out without deleting anything, use **Disconnetti account ("Log out")** instead.

If you have any question about what remains after deletion, write to us at the address below and we will answer.
